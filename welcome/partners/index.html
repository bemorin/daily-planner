Questions

Something else? Ask about the partner program.

Do I need Plus to become a partner?

No. Anyone can apply. Plus members often make the best partners, though: they know what they’re recommending.

How and when am I paid?

By Creem, our payment partner and the seller on every receipt. Creem pays partners on the 1st and the 15th of each month, once your balance reaches its minimum payout, and lists your sales and earnings in your partner dashboard.

How long does my link count?

60 days. The welcome page keeps your code on the visitor’s device, so if they come back later and buy Plus there or in the planner, the sale is still credited to you.

What do the people I recommend see?

The same welcome page and the same prices. Your code only tells Creem whom to credit. Their planner stays private and on their own device: no account, and nothing about them is shared with you.

Where do I see my clicks and sales?

In your Creem partner dashboard, with your earnings and payouts.

Can I recommend the team gift to a company?

Yes. Send them the welcome page with your code: a team gift bought online through your link counts like any other purchase.

Do my own purchases count?

No. Purchases you make yourself through your own link, for yourself or as a gift, don’t earn a commission. Creem tracks and pays commissions under its affiliate terms; this is the rule of our program.
