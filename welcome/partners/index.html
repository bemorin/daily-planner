The rules, plainly.

What counts, when it counts, and how you’re paid. The full text is in our terms.

  • 25% of Plus and personal gifts

    Daily Planner Plus, Give Plus and Plus Family bought through your link. Creem tracks these sales and pays you.

  • 10% of a team gift you introduced

    Bought online through your link, or by invoice when the company gives us your code. We pay it ourselves.

  • 30 days per click

    Your code stays on the visitor’s device for 30 days. If they followed several partners’ links, the latest one counts.

  • Fair play

    No spam, no ads on the Daily Planner name, no made-up discounts or promises. We may end a partnership that breaks these rules.

The program, explained.

Something else? Ask a question about the partner program.

How much is a sale worth?

25% of the price before tax of Plus, Give Plus and Plus Family. One Plus bought in France (€29) earns you €6.04. A team gift you introduced earns you 10%: €49.58 for a team of up to 50 people.

Who can be a partner?

Anyone: coaches, creators, newsletters, consultants, teams and people who simply like the planner. You don’t need Plus, there are no targets, and joining is free.

How are sales tracked?

Your link adds your code to the welcome page’s address. The visitor’s browser keeps it for 30 days and sends it with a purchase, on the welcome page or in the planner, so Creem credits you. If they followed several partners’ links, the latest one counts. Creem lists your clicks and sales in your partner dashboard.

When and how am I paid?

A sale is earned once its 14-day refund period has passed with no refund or dispute. Creem pays your 25% commissions on the 1st and the 15th of each month, once your balance reaches €50, after a short hold set by its payment partners. We pay team commissions by bank transfer, within 30 days of being earned, and contact you for the details.

What if someone asks for a refund?

A refunded or disputed sale earns nothing. That’s why a sale only counts once its 14-day refund period is over. A partial refund lowers the commission in proportion.

What is not allowed?

Spam, ads that bid on “Daily Planner” or look like ours, coupon and cashback sites, and promises or discounts we don’t make. Purchases you make yourself, for you or as a gift, don’t count. We may end a partnership that breaks these rules, and sales made that way earn nothing.

And companies?

Send them to the Teams page with your link. A team gift bought online through it earns you 10%. If the company prefers an invoice, they ask for it on the Teams page: your code goes with the request when they came through your link, or they can name you in their message.

What do the people I recommend see?

The same pages and the same prices: your link costs them nothing more. Their planner stays private, on their own device, with no account. Nothing about who bought is shared with you.
