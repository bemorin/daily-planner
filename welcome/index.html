One small loop, every day

Plan in the morning, work from your schedule, close the day in the evening. It takes a few minutes, and nothing unfinished gets lost.

  1. 1

    Plan

    Write one thing to keep in mind, choose the most important outcome and up to two others, then give them time on your schedule.

  2. 2

    Do

    Work from the schedule. Tick outcomes, tasks and time blocks as they get done.

  3. 3

    Close

    Review what got done and decide where every unfinished item goes. Then keep a memory and a lesson from the day.

Your week, in blocks

Week view puts your planning days side by side with their time blocks. It’s the same schedule as the Day view, so nothing needs copying.

  • Pick your planning days: Monday to Friday, or whichever days fit your life.
  • Click an empty slot to add a block, click a block to edit it.
  • Today keeps its current-time line; past days step back.

The planner

Do I need an account?

No. Open the planner and start. There’s no sign-up, no email and no password.

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything in the planner except the Plus features is free, for as long as you use it: no trial, no account and no card. Plus is an optional one-time purchase, and companies can give it to their people. That’s what pays for the planner.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser, on your device. It isn’t sent to our servers. You can back up everything to a file at any time and restore it in another browser.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once the planner has loaded, it reopens without a connection, which is especially handy when it’s installed as an app.

Is Google Drive sync safe?

It’s optional. When you connect your Google account, the planner can only reach its own hidden sync file in your Drive, not your other files. Your plans travel between your devices and your Drive; nothing is stored on our servers. Before applying changes from another device, the planner keeps a few recovery snapshots on your device.

Which devices does it work on?

Any recent browser on iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, Windows or Linux. To install it as an app, use Install in Chrome or Edge, or Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone and iPad.

Team gift

How do seats work?

You get one gift link. Each person who opens it activates Plus for life on their own planner, and each activation uses one seat. Your manage page shows how many seats are used and lets you change the message people see. Nobody signs up and no names are collected, so seats aren’t assigned to people: whoever opens the link first gets one. Share it only with the people it’s for. A small margin covers people who set the planner up again on a new device.

Does the company see what people plan?

No. Each person plans in their own planner, in their own browser. The gift only unlocks Plus: there’s no team account, no dashboard and no list of who activated it, so there’s nothing for the company, or for us, to look at.

Can we pay by invoice?

Yes. Choose a team size and send the request: you’ll receive an invoice to pay by bank transfer, then the gift link once it’s paid. It’s one payment, with no subscription.

Which currencies do you charge in?

Euros in the euro area and US dollars elsewhere, with the same numbers. The page picks one from your browser’s region; the EUR / USD switch above the prices changes it.

How long does the gift link work?

People can activate it for 12 months after it’s created. Once someone has activated Plus, it stays on their planner for life.
