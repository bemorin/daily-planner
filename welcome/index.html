One small loop, every day

Plan in the morning, work from your schedule, close the day in the evening. It takes a few minutes, and nothing unfinished gets lost.

  1. 1

    Plan

    Write one thing to keep in mind, choose the most important outcome and up to two others, then give them time on your schedule.

    Do

    Work from the schedule. Tick outcomes, tasks and time blocks as they get done.

  3. 3

    Close

    Review what got done and decide where every unfinished item goes. Then keep a memory and a lesson from the day.

      The planner

      Do I need an account?

      No. Open the planner and start. There’s no sign-up, no email and no password.

      Is it really free?

      Yes. Everything in the planner except the Plus features is free, for as long as you use it: no trial, no account and no card. Plus is optional, paid once at its launch price, and companies can give it to their people. That’s what pays for the planner.

      Where is my data stored?

      In your browser, on your device. It isn’t sent to our servers. You can back up everything to a file at any time and restore it in another browser.

      Does it work offline?

      Yes. Once the planner has loaded, it reopens without a connection, which is especially handy when it’s installed as an app.

      Is Google Drive sync safe?

      It’s optional. When you connect your Google account, the planner can only reach its own hidden sync file in your Drive, not your other files. Your plans travel between your devices and your Drive; nothing is stored on our servers. Before applying changes from another device, the planner keeps a few recovery snapshots on your device.

      Can I sync without Google?

      Not at the moment. Google Drive sync is free: one hidden file in your own Drive keeps your devices in step, and nothing is stored on our servers. Without it, a backup file moves everything from one browser to another.

      Can I share my week or my year?

      Yes, for free. When you close the last planning day of a week, its summary offers Share your week, and Progress offers Share your year. Either becomes a clean image for a post or a message, made on your device: nothing is uploaded.

      Which devices does it work on?

      Any recent browser on iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, Windows or Linux. To install it as an app, use Install in Chrome or Edge, or Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone and iPad.

      Privacy

      What this page counts and stores

      Your plans are never part of it: they stay in your browser.

      Anonymous counts

      To learn what helps, this page adds one to a daily count when someone:

        No identifiers, no cookies, your IP address is not stored.

        Prices and your country

        Euros in the euro area, US dollars elsewhere. To choose, the server reads your connection’s country (from Cloudflare) for that one request, and never stores it.

        Purchases

        • You pay on the page of Creem, our payment partner. Your card details never reach this site.
        • A purchase sends only what you chose, to open that page. If you have Plus in this browser, a gift also sends your licence, so the server can give you the member price; it’s checked, not stored.
        • If you came through a partner’s link, its referral code (?ref=) is kept in this browser for 30 days and sent with a purchase to credit that partner. Once you’ve paid, the code is kept with the sale, so we can see which partners bring sales. It names the partner, not you.

        Requests

        The request form on the Teams page sends what you type — your email, name, company, the kind of request, plan, number of people, currency, timing and message; for a question, only your email, name and question. It’s stored only to reply to you, with your browser’s language, the page version and where the form was opened, and deleted on request.

        If you came through a partner’s link, its code goes with the request too, so the partner can be credited for a team gift they introduced.
