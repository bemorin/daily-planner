Buying

Can we pay by invoice?

Yes. Choose a team size and send the request: you’ll receive an invoice to pay by bank transfer, then the gift link once it’s paid. It’s one payment, with no subscription.

Are taxes included, and what does the invoice show?

The prices shown include taxes. Bought online, Creem, our payment partner, is the merchant of record: it charges the VAT that applies where you are and emails the receipt and the invoice. By invoice, the invoice states the price, the taxes and the payment terms; add what it needs, such as your billing address, VAT number or purchase order number, under “Anything else?” in the request.

When do we get the gift link?

Bought online, right away: you land on your gift page as soon as it’s paid, and its link arrives by email with a message ready to forward. By invoice, once the invoice is paid: your gift page arrives by email. Either way, you forward the link when it suits you, for instance just before the holidays.

Can we start with a quote?

Yes. Choose Quote in the request form, with the number of people, and the quote follows by email. For more than 1,000 people, the team gift always starts with a quote.

Seats and people

How do seats work?

You get one gift link. Each person who opens it activates Plus for life on their own planner, and each activation uses one seat. Your gift page shows how many seats are used and lets you change the message people see. Nobody signs up and no names are collected, so seats aren’t assigned to people: whoever opens the link first gets one. Share it only with the people it’s for. A small margin covers people who set the planner up again on a new device.

What does each person get?

Daily Planner Plus for life, on their own planner, as if they had bought it themselves. It’s theirs: they keep it if they change jobs.

  • Plan your year. A guided half hour, printed on one page.
  • Quarterly check-ins. Ten guided minutes as each quarter ends.
  • Insights. Their best weekdays, when their focus happens and where their hours go.
  • Your year in review. One printable page for the whole year.
  • Colours for your painted year. Five more palettes.
  • What comes next. Everything Plus gets later, at no extra cost.
How long does the gift link work?

People can activate it for 12 months after it’s created. Once someone has activated Plus, it stays on their planner for life.

What if someone joins or leaves?

Someone who joins later opens the same link, as long as seats remain and the link is still open. Someone who leaves keeps Plus: it’s on their own planner, and there’s nothing to take back. Seats aren’t assigned to names, so there’s nothing to reassign either.

What if we need more seats?

When every seat is used, the link stops working. For more people, buy another team gift, which comes with its own link, or ask us.

Can people use Plus on several devices?

Yes. Each person opens the link once, on the device where they plan. Plus is saved in their planner’s settings, so Google Drive sync and backups carry it to their other devices.

Privacy

Does the company see what people plan?

No. Each person plans in their own planner, in their own browser. The gift only unlocks Plus: there’s no team account, no dashboard and no list of who activated it, so there’s nothing for the company, or for us, to look at.

Do you collect our people’s names or emails?

No. Nobody signs up: opening the link activates Plus in that person’s browser, and our server only counts the activation. We use the buyer’s email, from the payment or the request form, to send the gift link and the invoice, and nothing about the people who use it.

Privacy

What this page counts and stores

Your plans are never part of it: they stay in your browser.

Anonymous counts

To learn what helps, this page adds one to a daily count when someone:

    No identifiers, no cookies, your IP address is not stored.

    A visit through a partner’s link (?ref=) is counted once a day, as a daily total per partner code: only the code is sent, with nothing about you, and that partner sees the totals on their private statement. Only a code we know is counted, and nothing is sent when you switch the counts off.

    Prices and your country

    Euros in the euro area, US dollars elsewhere. To choose, the server reads your connection’s country (from Cloudflare) for that one request, and never stores it.

    Purchases

    • You pay on the page of Creem, our payment partner. Your card details never reach this site.
    • A purchase sends only what you chose, to open that page. If you have Plus in this browser, a gift also sends your licence, so the server can give you the member price; it’s checked, not stored.
    • If you came through a partner’s link, its referral code (?ref=) is kept in this browser for 30 days and sent with a purchase to credit that partner. Once you’ve paid, the code is kept with the sale, so we can see which partners bring sales. It names the partner, not you.

    Requests

    The Teams page’s request form sends what you type — your email, name, company, the kind of request, plan, number of people, currency, timing and message; a question, asked there or on the Contact page, only your email, name and question. It’s stored only to reply to you and follow up, with your browser’s language, the page version and where the form was opened, and deleted whenever you ask.

    If you came through a partner’s link, its code goes with the request too, so the partner can be credited for a team gift they introduced.
