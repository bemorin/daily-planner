      1. Pick outcomes that matter

      Give one outcome visual priority, then keep only two other important outcomes. The hierarchy should remain obvious at a glance.

      2. Make every task verifiable

      Too vagueWork on the reportDraft the introduction and section 1 (max 3 pages)
      Too vagueTidy the deskDiscard old papers from the top drawer
      Too vagueEmailsProcess the inbox and reach Inbox Zero

      3. Put work on the schedule

      Click an empty slot to create a block. Click an existing block to edit it. Hold briefly and drag to move it; resize from the lower edge. The calendar action beside a task schedules that specific item. Start and End accept typed times or 15-minute menus, and a block can cross midnight for up to 24 hours.

      4. Categories and statistics

      Categories can represent projects or areas of life. Choose from three distinct color palettes, then archive a category when it is no longer active: its historical blocks keep their identity, but it disappears from new planning. Archived categories remain visible in Settings and can be restored. Day and Week statistics are derived from scheduled blocks.

      5. Choose the schedule range

      Settings lets you choose the hours you want visible and the days you normally plan. Week view shows those planning days side by side; Close day uses them to find the next planning day. Inactive days are never blocked — you can still open or schedule them manually.

      6. Close the day deliberately

      Every unfinished item can go to a specific date, be Kept safely without a date in Unresolved, or be Dropped. The carried count and original date remain attached so repeated postponement stays visible.

      7. Keep the horizon visible

      Horizon keeps the year visible without turning it into another task manager, using a yearly rhythm inspired by Jesse Itzler: one Misogi period, three meaningful commitments, up to six Mini-adventures, quarterly Winning habits and significant Key dates grouped in the Year map. Winning habits are checked when you close the day; their activity stays available on demand.

      8. Find and change your view

      Search scans saved planner days. Press F outside a text field. Fit app to width enlarges only this planner — it never changes browser zoom for the rest of your domain.

      9. Floating mode & print

      Picture-in-Picture keeps a compact editable Plan / Schedule view while you work elsewhere. Print is optimized for a landscape planner sheet and hides editing controls.

      10. Install it without creating an account

      Use your browser’s install action when it is available. On iPhone or iPad in Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen. In Chromium browsers, look for Install app in the address bar or browser menu. The installed app uses the same local planner data and can work offline after the production app has been loaded once.
