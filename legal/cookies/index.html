Cookies and storage

Daily Planner sets no cookies. Your browser does keep some data so the planner can work — mostly your planner itself. Here is all of it.

No cookies

The planner, the welcome page and these pages set no cookies, and load no advertising, analytics or tracking scripts from other companies. The anonymous counts described in the privacy policy use no cookies either.

Our host, Cloudflare, can set a strictly necessary security cookie in some cases — for example __cf_bm when bot protection is on, or cf_clearance after a security check. They protect the site and are not used to follow you (Cloudflare’s list).

What your browser keeps

The planner saves data in your browser’s storage, on your device. None of it is sent to us, with one exception: if you have Plus, the planner sends your licence about once a week to check that it is still valid (see the privacy policy). Keys that start with daily-planner: are your planner: they go into backups and, if you use it, Google Drive sync. The others belong to this browser only.

The offline copy

When you open the planner, a service worker saves its files in your browser’s cache so it opens and works without a connection. Each new version replaces the previous copy. It holds the app and pages of this site, not your plans.

How to clear it

  • Your planner: in the planner, open Help → Data on this device → Reset local data. Export a backup first if you may want it later.
  • Everything this site keeps, including your preferences and the offline copy: clear the site data for this address in your browser’s settings (usually under Privacy or Site settings). Your planner is deleted too, so back it up first.
  • Google Drive sync: disconnect in the planner’s Settings first. The copy in your Drive stays until you delete it there (Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Daily Planner → Delete hidden app data).

Google and Creem

If you connect Google Drive, the planner loads Google’s sign-in and Google’s window opens on Google’s own pages, where Google uses its own cookies under its own policy. Nothing from Google loads before you choose Connect.

When you buy Plus or a gift, you pay on Creem’s checkout page, where Creem’s own policies apply (Creem’s privacy policy).
