Plus Family

Plus Family gives Plus to up to 5 people who live in the same household, for their personal, non-commercial use.

You receive one gift link. Each person opens it once on the device where they plan, and each activation uses one of the 5 places. For colleagues or a company, use a team gift instead.

Gifts and team gifts

A gift — Give Plus for one person, Plus Family, or a team gift for a company — is one link. Each activation uses one place, or “seat”; when every place has been used, the link stops working. Each person who activates a gift gets Plus on the same terms as a personal licence.

Gift links can be activated for 12 months from the purchase. Plus stays on wherever it was activated.

Team gifts include a small margin from 10 seats: 10% more activations (at least one), for people who set the planner up again. Smaller gifts allow exactly the places bought.

If you already have Plus, Give Plus and Plus Family are offered at a lower member price. When you buy one, the planner or the welcome page sends your licence with the checkout, so we can check that it is valid and not refunded; the price you pay is shown on the checkout before you pay. Team gifts have no member price.

You receive the gift link and a private page where you can see how many places have been used and edit the message and name shown with the gift. Share the link only with the people the gift is for: anyone who has it can use a place.

If your company pays a team gift by invoice, the invoice states the price, taxes and payment terms; everything else on this page applies in the same way.
