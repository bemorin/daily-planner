Privacy policy

Daily Planner keeps your plans on your device. This page explains the little the site does receive, why, how long it is kept, and how to reach us.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

In short

  • Your plans, journal and settings stay in your browser. We never receive them, and there is no account.
  • The site counts a few anonymous moments per day, with no cookies and no identifiers, and you can switch that off.
  • When you send us something — a waitlist sign-up, feedback, a request or a purchase — we keep what you sent for that purpose only, and delete it when you ask, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • We don’t sell data, show ads or use trackers from other companies.

What stays on your device

Your plans, tasks, notes, journal, Horizon, statistics and settings are saved in your browser, on your device. They are never sent to us — not even with feedback.

Backups and exports are files you download and keep yourself. If you clear your browser’s data for this site, your planner goes with it, so keep a backup.

Daily Planner Plus is checked on your device too: the licence is saved with your planner settings. About once a week, when you are online, the planner also asks us whether that licence is still valid, so Plus can be switched off after a refund; it sends only the licence (see Daily Planner Plus and gifts). Cookies and storage lists everything your browser keeps for the planner.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn which parts of Daily Planner are used, the planner and its welcome page add one to a daily count when someone does one of the things below. A count holds only the name of the moment, the app version and the day. There is no identifier and no cookie, your IP address is not stored, and nothing you write is included.

A few moments, such as opening the planner, are counted at most once per day, week or month on a device, so we can tell roughly how many devices use the planner without identifying any of them. Your browser notes when it last counted each one; that note never leaves your device.

      Feedback and reviews

      When you send feedback from the planner, we store your rating, the kind of note (idea, problem, praise or other), your message, the app version, the kind of device (for example “Mac · browser”) and the date — and your email address only if you add one, so we can reply.

      If you tick “You can quote this on the Daily Planner website”, your rating, your message and the name you chose to give (optional) are also kept as a review that may be published there. Your email address is never shown. Ask, and we remove the review.

      We keep feedback to improve the planner and to reply, and delete it on request.

      Team requests and questions

      The Teams page’s request form stores, for an invoice or a quote, your work email, your name, your company, the number of people, the plan and currency shown, the kind of request, when you would like to start and your message. A question, asked there or on the Contact page, stores your email, your name and your question. With either, it stores your browser language, the app version, where you sent it from (the website or the planner) and the date.

      If you came through a partner’s link in the last 30 days, the partner’s code goes with your request, so a team order by invoice can earn that partner their commission. It names the partner, not you, and the partner doesn’t learn who asked.

      We use it only to answer your request, never to send you anything else, so there is no box to tick. We receive an email notification with your request, and keep it to reply and follow up. Ask, and we delete it.

      Daily Planner Plus and gifts

      Prices

      Prices are shown in euros in the euro area and in US dollars elsewhere. To choose, our server reads the country of your connection, as Cloudflare reports it, for that one request. It is not stored.

      Paying

      You pay on the checkout page of Creem, our merchant of record: Creem sells Plus and gifts to you, takes the payment, charges taxes and sends your receipt. What you enter there — your email address, name, billing details and payment details — goes to Creem and is handled under Creem’s privacy policy. Your card details never reach us.

      To open the checkout, our server tells Creem what you are buying, its price and currency and, for a gift, its plan, its number of places and whether the member price applies — with a partner’s code if you came through a partner’s link.

      The member price

      If you already have Plus and buy Give Plus or Plus Family, the planner or the welcome page sends your licence with the checkout, so our server can check its signature and that it was not refunded before charging the member price. The licence is checked for that request only and is not stored, and it is never sent to Creem: Creem only learns that the member price applied.

      What we keep after a purchase

      When the payment is confirmed, Creem sends us the details of the checkout, and we keep:

      • For Plus: the checkout reference, a random licence id, how many times the purchase has been activated, your email address and the date.
      • For a gift: the gift code, the name shown with it (your company, or your name for a personal gift, if you typed one), its message, the number of places and how many have been used, the amount and currency, the checkout reference, your email address, and when it was created, emailed and expires. Nothing is kept about the people who activate it: only a counter.
      • For every payment, a sales record: what was bought, the amount, the currency, your country as Creem reports it, and the date — with no name and no email address.
      • For every paid checkout, Creem’s order reference, so a later refund can be linked to what it paid for.
      • For a purchase made through a partner’s link, the partner’s code with the sale: what was bought, the amount, the tax included in it and the currency, whether the member price applied and the date, so we can see which partners bring sales and work out their commission, and — for a team gift, whose commission we pay ourselves — when we paid it. The code names the partner, not you, and it is kept with the sale’s record, under the same checkout reference as your purchase.

      Your email address is used to send you the link that activates Plus on your devices, or your gift link and the private page where you manage the gift. Replies to those emails come to us.

      Refunds and disputes

      When Creem tells us that a payment was refunded, or disputed with a bank, we keep Creem’s reference for it, the amount and currency, whether it was a full refund, the reason Creem gives, the order and checkout it belongs to, the date, and what was switched off. The buyer’s email address Creem sends with it is not stored in our database: it only appears in the notification email we send ourselves about the refund, through Resend, our email provider.

      A full refund or a dispute switches Plus off: the purchase link stops working, a gift link is switched off, and the licences they gave are added to a list of revoked licences (a purchase refunded before it was delivered is then never delivered). That list holds only licence ids, checkout and order references and a code for each gift — never who used them.

      Your licence

      Your licence is a signed code saved with your planner settings, so backups and Google Drive sync carry it to your other devices. It contains a random id, where it came from (a purchase, a gift or a licence we made), the day it was made and, for a gift, the name shown with the gift and a code that identifies the gift without revealing its link — nothing else about you. Activating a gift sends us only the gift code; confirming a purchase sends only the checkout reference.

      About once a week, when you are online, the planner sends us only the licence (with its random id) to ask whether it is still valid, nothing else: no app data, nothing from your planner. If the purchase or the gift was refunded or cancelled, Plus is switched off on that planner and a short message says why; the planner keeps the licence on that device and asks again each week, so Plus comes back if that is undone. We don’t keep anything about these checks. If the planner is offline or can’t reach us, Plus simply stays as it is.

      Partner links

      If you arrive through a partner’s link (one with ?ref= and a code), your browser keeps that code for 30 days and sends it with a checkout, or with a team request, so the partner is credited. For Plus, Give Plus and Plus Family we pass it to Creem; for a team gift we keep it ourselves, as we pay that commission. Once you have paid we keep it with the sale (see above). It names the partner, not you. Partners join, see their sales and are paid through Creem’s affiliate program, under Creem’s privacy policy; they never learn who bought.

      Your browser also tells us when you arrive through a partner’s link, at most once a day for each code: it sends only the code, and we add one to that code’s count for the day — only for a partner code we know (one that brought a sale, or one we gave a partner). We keep these daily totals per partner code, with nothing about you (no address, no identifier), for about 13 months, so the partner can see on their private statement how many visits their link brings. Nothing is sent when you have switched the anonymous counts off.

      Google Drive sync

      Optional and free. When you choose Connect in the planner’s settings, Google asks you to let Daily Planner use its own hidden app folder in your Google Drive. The planner then keeps one hidden file there, written and read directly by your browser. Your planner never passes through our servers or stays on them, and we never see your Google account or your other files.

      The access Google grants is kept only in the open page. Google’s sign-in and the file in your Drive are covered by Google’s privacy policy. Nothing reaches Google until you choose Connect. To stop, disconnect in Settings; you can also remove Daily Planner’s access in your Google Account and delete its hidden data in Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps).

      Emails

      The site sends emails through Resend, our email provider, and only for these reasons: the link that activates Plus after a purchase, a gift link with its private page, and the one waitlist email. We also get an email when a team request arrives, and when Creem reports a refund or a dispute. When we reply to you, we write from our own mailbox. We send no newsletters and no marketing.

      Your rights

      Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

      • show you the personal data we hold about you, and give you a copy in a format you can reuse;
      • correct it, or delete it;
      • limit how we use it, or object to it — including the usage counts, which you can also switch off yourself;
      • stop using it where you gave your consent (the waitlist, a quoted review), at any time.

      To use any of these rights, use the “Ask a question” form. We answer within one month, and may ask you to confirm the email address the data is linked to. Your planner itself is only on your device, so only you can see or delete it there.

      If you think we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr), or to the authority of the country where you live.
