How to fix it

  • On this computer — stop the server and start it with the API: npm run start:api, then open the /admin/ address it prints.
  • In production (Cloudflare Pages) — bind the D1 database as DB, add the encrypted secret ADMIN_TOKEN, set the build variable DAILY_PLANNER_API_URL=/api, then redeploy. Step by step: docs/FEEDBACK_AND_WAITLIST.md.

    Refunds and disputes

    What Creem reports. A full refund or a dispute switches Plus off: the purchase link stops working, or the gift link with every activation made from it. Planners lose Plus at their next weekly check, and get it back the same way after Restore. A partial refund only emails you.

    Revoke Plus by hand

    For a refund that matched no purchase, a dispute settled outside Creem, or a licence made here. A checkout id works before its purchase is delivered too. Restore undoes it.

    Partners

    Sales made through a partner’s link (?ref=CODE), recorded when the checkout is paid. Creem pays partners 25% of Plus and personal gifts (shown here for information; Creem’s own figures and payouts are in Creem → Growth → Affiliate Hub). You pay 10% of team gifts by bank transfer, once a sale’s 14-day refund period has passed: check a new code in Creem first, then mark each one as paid here. An individual gets at most €300 / $300 of team commissions a calendar year; a business (micro-entreprise or company) invoices you, with no cap. Each partner’s private statement shows their visits and commissions: copy its link here.
